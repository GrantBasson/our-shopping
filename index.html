Our Shopping

Your household. Your Google Drive.

A simple shared shopping list for Android. Sign in with Google, create a household Sheet that you own, and invite the people you shop with.

  • Add, edit and check off items together.
  • Remember past item names and record optional unit prices.
  • Keep your shopping data in your household owner’s Google Drive.

Open Our Shopping

Google sign-in and file authorization are required. The Android app opens the list through a secure browser app tab. Google account and device policies may affect availability. Free Google service quotas apply.

This application is independently developed. It is not a Google product and does not claim Google verification until a review has actually been completed.
