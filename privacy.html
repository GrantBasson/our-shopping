Privacy policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026.

Our Shopping is operated by Grant Basson. Contact: g.d.basson@gmail.com.

Information used

Google handles sign-in and authorization. The app uses your Google account email to identify you, display your current account and check household ownership/access. It processes the shopping items, quantities, categories, prices, timestamps and history you store in a household Sheet.

Where your data lives

The household’s creating Google account owns its Sheet in My Drive. Invited members access that same Sheet; other households use separate Sheets. The developer does not maintain a central shopping database. Google Apps Script runs the backend and processes authorized requests. Account-specific preferences on that service store Sheet IDs/names, the selected household and recoverable setup receipts.

Your browser stores cached shopping data, drafts and pending operations on your device for reliability. Those copies remain until you remove them. Use trusted devices. Access revocation cannot erase copies someone already downloaded or cached.

Google API permissions

The app requests access only to Drive files you create or explicitly select with Google Picker, plus your account email. That file permission is used to read/write the shopping Sheet and let its owner grant/revoke editor access. The backend contacts Google’s Drive and Sheets APIs. Google Picker temporarily receives the accessing user’s short-lived authorization token to select an allowed file. The app does not store that token in browser storage and never receives your Google password.

Sharing and AI

Household owners choose whom to invite. An invitation grants the specified Google account editor access to that Sheet. We do not sell shopping or account data, use it for advertising, or send it to AI services. Future integrations are not automatically enabled; sharing with another tool requires a separate explicit action and its own disclosures. Our Shopping’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Shopping history, including removed items, stays in the owner’s Sheet until the owner deletes it or restores an earlier version. Disconnecting a household removes its saved connection, not its Sheet or Google sharing. Owners can remove members in the app or in Drive. Users can revoke the app at Google Account permissions. Removing the Android app does not delete the owner’s Sheet or browser data. Clear the app’s site storage in your browser to remove cached copies, but resolve pending saves first. Contact the operator to request removal of server-side app preferences. Removing preferences does not delete files owned by you or other users.

Operational data and changes

The app does not add advertising or analytics trackers. Google may process operational data under its own terms, and service errors may be recorded in Google-hosted logs for diagnosis. The app does not intentionally log access tokens or shopping contents. Material changes to access or data use will be disclosed before enabling them. Contact the operator with privacy questions.
