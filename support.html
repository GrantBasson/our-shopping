Support

Contact Grant Basson at g.d.basson@gmail.com for Our Shopping support or privacy requests. Include the error message and app version. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, signing keys or your whole shopping Sheet.

Common connection problems

Use the Google account that the household owner invited. Select the shared household Sheet in Google Picker to grant the app per-file access; possession of a link alone grants no permission. If Google is temporarily unavailable, retain pending changes and retry later.

Household data belongs to the Sheet owner. Disconnecting the app does not delete that Sheet or remove existing Drive sharing.
